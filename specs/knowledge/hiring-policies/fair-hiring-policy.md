# Fair and explainable hiring policy

## Purpose
This policy defines how AgentHire evaluates applicants so that every recommendation is job-related,
consistent and explainable to the recruiter who makes the final decision.

## Job-related criteria only
Candidates are evaluated only against the requirements recorded on the job: required skills, preferred
skills and minimum years of professional experience. Evidence must come from the resume content:
roles held, responsibilities, technologies used and projects delivered.

The following must never influence an evaluation: name, age or date of birth, gender, race or ethnicity,
nationality or citizenship, religion, marital or family status, pregnancy, disability or health, sexual
orientation, political affiliation, photographs, home address and graduation year used as an age proxy.
Gaps in employment are not negative evidence on their own.

## Evidence standard
A skill counts as demonstrated when the resume shows the candidate used it, for example in a role,
a project, or a described achievement. A bare keyword list is weaker evidence than described use, but
it still counts as a mention. Do not assume a skill from a related one (for example, React experience
does not prove TypeScript experience).

## Human decision
AI output is a recommendation. A recruiter reviews every candidate, including those the AI scores
below the hold threshold, and may override any recommendation with a recorded reason.
