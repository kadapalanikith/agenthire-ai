# Structured interview guidelines

## Principles
Ask every candidate for the same role the same core questions, in the same order, and score answers
against a written rubric. Questions must relate to the job's required and preferred skills.

## Question types
- **Experience questions** ask about something the candidate has done: "Tell me about a component you built
  that had to handle complex state."
- **Situational questions** present a realistic problem: "A page renders slowly on low-end phones. How would you investigate?"
- **Technical exercises** are short, time-boxed and representative of the job's daily work.

## Rubric levels
Use four levels for every question: does not meet, partially meets, meets, exceeds. Describe observable
behaviour for each level before the interview.

## Prohibited topics
Never ask about age, family plans, marital status, religion, nationality, health, disability or any other
protected characteristic, or questions that act as proxies for them.

## Candidate experience
Explain the format at the start, allow clarifying questions, and give candidates time to finish their answers.
